I'm **Darshan RG**, a software developer whose path moved from formal engineering training and industry roles into full-stack work and, more recently, sustained study of how people experience intelligent systems.

I care about how software is built and how it is *lived with* — especially through generative AI and **Human–AI Interaction (HAI)**: where technical systems meet interpretation, habit, and meaning.

This site documents how that engineering trajectory opened into **independent, research-oriented exploration** of dialogue, cognition, language, and observable model behavior over long interactions.

## Toward sustained HAI work

Moving into software engineering made it possible to work closely with real products and teams. That foundation later became the ground for a different question: not only what models output, but **how understanding, interpretation, and meaning change** when humans and language models stay in dialogue over time.

The [research archive](/orientation/purpose-and-boundaries/) collects that thread — methods, concepts, field notes, and frameworks in progress.
