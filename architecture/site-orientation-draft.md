# Independent Research & Explorations (site draft)

_Human–AI Interaction · Cognition · Phenomenology · Philosophy · Systems Thinking_

This space documents independent inquiry into human–AI interaction, cognition, language, meaning-making and observable behavior of language models.

It grew from more than two years of sustained interaction with large language models — studying how meaning, context, reasoning and conceptual structures evolve across **extended** human–AI dialogue.

Independent intellectual workspace in evolving archive form.

---

## Central inquiry

**How does meaning, understanding and reasoning evolve through sustained interaction between humans and artificial intelligence systems?**

---

## Research orientation (summary)

1. **Human–AI Interaction** — mental models, continuity, instruction design, semantic alignment, collaborative inquiry  
2. **Phenomenology & first-person inquiry** — sense-making, interpretation, adaptation through extended dialogue  
3. **Cognition & philosophy of mind** — interpreted with care about what dialogue alone can support  
4. **Language, meaning & symbolic structures** — coherence, drift, mapping, multi-perspectival reasoning  
5. **Observable AI behavior** — traces, consistency, hallucinations, conflicts, context effects  
6. **Human-centered AI** — evaluation, interface and methodology informed by sustained use  

---

## Methods (combined)

Longitudinal observation · first-person participatory inquiry · reflective qualitative analysis · interaction trace analysis · structured prompt experimentation · semantic pattern analysis · multi-perspectival interpretation · framework development · interdisciplinary reading.

Methodology is evolving; explorations are labeled by evidential status where relevant.
