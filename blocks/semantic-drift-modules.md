## 01 · Accessible

Gradual change in meaning, interpretation or conversational direction as interaction continues.

## 02 · Philosophical

How is meaning maintained, transformed or reconstructed across temporal sequences of dialogue?

## 03 · Technical (observable)

Changes in response relevance, instruction adherence, reference consistency and contextual interpretation across multi-turn interactions.

## 04 · Methodological

Compare initial intentions, intermediate conversational states and later responses to identify observable shifts.

## 05 · Open question

How can semantic continuity be operationalized and measured across long conversational sequences?
