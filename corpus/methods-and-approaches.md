## HAI-focused methodology (compressed)

Investigation through sustained observational methods: how users develop mental models and reasoning strategies in **extended** LLM dialogue — emphasis on long-context dynamics and semantic alignment challenges.

Analyzing (among other objects):

- Prompt–response trajectories across threads  
- Behavioral consistency in reasoning chains  
- Context degradation in multi-turn exchange  
- Anomaly patterns relevant to instruction adherence  

Bridges qualitative observation with structured pattern recognition — centered on **observable behavior and context**.
