Seven dimensions of the same evolving inquiry — not seven separate disciplines.

## A. Human–AI Interaction (central observational domain)

How humans and language models interact, adapt, interpret, construct meaning and maintain continuity across extended dialogue.

## B. Phenomenology and first-person inquiry

First-person experience · participatory inquiry · autoethnography · sense-making · human mental models · experience of interacting with AI.

## C. Language, semantics and symbolic structures

Semantic coherence · meaning construction · semantic drift · conceptual representations · symbolic reasoning · adaptive vocabulary.

## D. AI behavior and interaction dynamics

Long-context behavior · response consistency · context degradation · instruction conflicts · hallucination analysis · observable alignment and misalignment.

## E. Cognitive science and philosophy of mind

Consciousness · cognition · perception · self-models · emergence · philosophy of mind — without inferring consciousness from chat behavior alone.

## F. Methods and epistemology

First-person observation · reflective analysis · interaction trace analysis · multi-perspectival interpretation · conceptual cartography · qualitative pattern recognition.

## G. Human-centered AI and interface design

Human-in-the-loop systems · mental models of AI · trust and interpretation · collaborative inquiry · observational interface design · translating insight into usable frameworks.
