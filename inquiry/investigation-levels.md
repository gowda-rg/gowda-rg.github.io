Material is tagged across three levels so readers can see which inferential moves are in play.

| Level | What is under investigation |
|-------|-----------------------------|
| **First-person** | What happens to human understanding during sustained AI interaction? |
| **Interaction-level** | What patterns emerge between human instructions, context and model responses? |
| **Conceptual / theoretical** | How might these patterns relate to cognition, consciousness, semantics and systems theory? |

Use level tags when adding new pages (field notes, frameworks, concepts) so readers know which inferential moves are in play.
