# Load-bearing functions (human in the loop)

In sustained human–AI interaction, the human side routinely supplies:

- Grounding  
- Goal coherence  
- Error correction  
- Value alignment  
- Context selection  
- Semantic pruning  

These are not auxiliary. They are **load-bearing**.

They are also what tends to fail when the human is removed or when interaction is scaled without equivalent structure.
