Independent inquiry published as an evolving open archive.

## Distinguish clearly

| Kind | Treatment in the archive |
|------|---------------------------|
| First-person observations | Record as observed |
| Interpretations | Label as interpretation |
| Conceptual models | Version; note limits |
| Working hypotheses | Provisional |
| Proposed methodologies | Descriptive |
| Technical experiments | Link to repos / notes |
| Empirically validated findings | Only when actually validated |

## On AI models

Claims in this archive stay at the level of:

- Observable responses
- Interaction traces
- Contextual behavior
- Inference limits when reasoning from behavior alone

## On expertise

Exploratory philosophical and interdisciplinary material is **labeled as such**; established scientific claims require cited support.

Preserve meaningful terminology; surface **ambiguity** instead of silent redefinition.
