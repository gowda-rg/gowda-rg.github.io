## Function of this space

`gowda-rg.github.io` is an **externalized intellectual workspace** and an **evolving map** of independent inquiry.

The work behind it is not one linear research project. It is an accumulation of:

- Intellectual practices
- Observational methods
- Conceptual vocabularies
- Working hypotheses
- Attempts to understand human–AI interaction from the inside

The aim is to externalize thinking so it can be **inspected, differentiated, reorganized, connected and developed** without depending on working memory alone.

Visitors may also use [About](/about/) for trajectory context and [platform ecosystem](platform-ecosystem.md) for how this domain connects to other publication spaces.

## Terminology

Retain terms such as **phenomenology**, **sense-making**, **autoethnography**, **symbolic representation**, **epistemology** and **cognitive cartography** when they accurately describe the inquiry.

Always separate **philosophical interpretation** from **empirical evidence**.
